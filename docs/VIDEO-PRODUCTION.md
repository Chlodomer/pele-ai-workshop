## Proposed production specification

- Desktop player: 16:9; target final 1920 × 1080; 24 fps. Prepare a low-resolution review preview before final export.
- 45–65 seconds per process. These are production estimates, not pedagogical requirements.
- One action or relationship per scene. Keep all text within a 10% safe area and use generous reading holds.
- Cream #F6F4EC, deep green #004128, gold #8B7029, olive #6B7A5E. Assistant for explanatory text and Karantina for short headings.
- Show the researcher's check as a real interruption in motion: movement pauses, the source is inspected, then progression resumes.
- No flashing, spinning, decorative particles or generic AI imagery. Graphics must explain the task.
- Native Higgsedit only. Inspect current CLI capabilities and type definitions before authoring. The previous render had mixed Hebrew/number shaping limitations; verify actual frames and use approved raster text layers if still required.
- Keep editable project, assets and rendering notes. Final video should use a native HTML video player with controls, no autoplay, optional soundtrack and an adjacent full text alternative.
- Prepare a silent review preview first. Add the user-supplied Suno track only after receiving it; keep a silent alternate export.

## Clip 01 — Preparing the collection (45–60 s)

0–7: ״מחומרי גלם לאוסף שאפשר לעבוד איתו״.
7–18: Two labelled practice documents move toward an index table. Original documents remain visible and unchanged.

## Clip 02 — Investigating across interviews (50–65 s)

0–8: One research question occupies the center.
8–22: Two interview labels feed source excerpts into an evidence table. Keep an explicit visible link between excerpt and source identifier.
22–36: Separate status labels appear: ״תומך״, ״סותר״, ״לא ברור״, ״לא נדון״. Never collapse them into one score.
36–49: Researcher checkpoint: return to the excerpt and its context before expanding.
49–59: The inspected evidence table becomes a comparison map. Do not invent numerical research results or show frequency as analytical importance.
59–65: Hold on ״הטבלה נבדקת לפני התרשים״.

## Clip 03 — Testing an argument (45–60 s)

0–8: ״טענה זמנית״ appears at center.
8–23: Evidence components connect from separate sides; keep supporting and contradictory material distinct using text as well as color.
23–35: An alternative explanation enters and changes the relationship map.
35–47: Movement pauses. ״מה צריך לחזור ולבדוק במקור?״
47–55: The researcher refines the claim; keep a limitation visible beside it.
55–60: Hold on ״ההחלטה נשארת אצל החוקר״.
